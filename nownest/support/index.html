Support

Help with NowNest

NowNest is in qualification and is not currently available for download. These answers describe the version now being tested.

Get in touch

For a question or qualification feedback, email support@foculoom.com. Response times are not guaranteed.

Questions people may have

What does NowNest do?

It keeps your current project, outcome, and next action visible. When another idea interrupts, you can save it for later and return to the task already in front of you.

What happens when I return to a saved idea?

NowNest shows the idea with the context it interrupted. Add one concrete starting action and choose Resume. Later, you can mark it Done, Save again, or Abandon it.

Does it need an account or internet connection?

No. The qualification build works offline with no account, cloud sync, or network access. These claims remain subject to release qualification.

Does NowNest collect my data?

The qualification build is designed not to collect, transmit, or share personal information. Website visits and messages sent to support are separate; the Privacy Policy explains both.

Can I download it?

Not yet. NowNest is not currently available for download. It still needs more hands-on use, exact-build checks, outside testing, and distribution review.

How do I remove my data?

Saved ideas can be marked Done, saved again, or Abandoned; the qualification build does not provide an individual hard-delete action. Removing the app is the available whole-store removal path. Device offload, backup, and restore behavior is controlled by the operating system.

What iOS version does the qualification build use?

The current target is iOS 18.0 or later on iPhone and iPad. Public release requirements are not yet established.
