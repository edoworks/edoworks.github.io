Frequently Asked Questions

What is NowNest?

NowNest is a quiet, offline-first space for holding your current next action when a new idea appears. You keep one project, outcome, and next action visible. When an interruption arrives, you park it locally and return to NOW.

Does NowNest need an account or internet connection?

No. NowNest works entirely offline. No account, no login, no cloud sync, no network access. All your data stays on your device.

Does NowNest collect my data?

No. NowNest does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. No analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Is NowNest free?

Yes. NowNest is free to download and use. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no paid features.

How do I delete my data?

Your data lives only on your device. To delete all data, simply uninstall the app. You can also delete individual parked ideas from the Review screen within the app.

What iOS version do I need?

NowNest requires iOS 18.0 or later. It is optimized for both iPhone and iPad.
