An idea can show up in the middle of anything. NowNest lets you save it without losing your place, then come back with the context you need to start.

Your notes stay with you

The version being tested has no account, cloud sync, analytics, notifications, or network access. Your NOW context and saved ideas stay on the device. Read the privacy details.

A small loop on purpose

Keep moving without dropping the thought.

NowNest does not ask you to reorganize your life. It protects the next action already in front of you.

1

Keep your place

Your project, outcome, and next action stay visible, so the work you meant to do does not disappear behind the interruption.

2

Save the interruption

Write the thought down once. NowNest saves it locally with the context it interrupted, then brings you back to NOW.

3

Pick it back up

Open a saved idea, choose one concrete starting action, and Resume. When you finish, mark it Done, Save again, or Abandon it.
