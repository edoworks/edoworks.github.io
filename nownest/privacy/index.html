Privacy

What stays on your device

Effective September 22, 2026 · Updated September 23, 2026

Qualification-stage scope: NowNest is in qualification and is not currently available for download. The tested build has no analytics, ads, third-party SDKs, accounts, or network access. Support messages and website access are separate from app data.

1. Who we are

NowNest is a provisional product name used by Foculoom LLC, a California limited liability company. Questions can be sent through the support page.

2. Information the app does not collect

The current qualification build works entirely offline:

  • Your NOW context and saved ideas stay on the device. The app does not send them to a server.
  • The app does not include analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or third-party SDKs.
  • The app does not create an account, profile you, or track you across apps or websites.
  • The app does not collect your name, email address, or contact information.
  • The app has no API calls, cloud sync, or remote storage.
  • The app does not schedule or send notifications.

3. Information we may process outside the app

  • Website access: GitHub Pages may retain standard web-server access logs under its own policies.
  • Support messages: If you email support, we receive what you choose to send and use it to respond.

4. Retention and removal

App information remains in local storage. Removing the app is the available whole-store removal path. Device offload, backup, and restore behavior is controlled by the operating system, so this policy does not promise irreversible deletion from every backup. Support messages are kept only as needed to answer the message or meet legal obligations.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information. Because the app does not send us personal information, these requests primarily concern support messages. Contact us to make a request.

6. Children’s privacy

NowNest is not directed at children. The qualification build does not collect personal information through the app.

7. Security

The tested build keeps app information local and has no server-side app database. Device security, backups, and operating-system protections remain outside NowNest.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as qualification or availability changes. Material changes will be noted here.
