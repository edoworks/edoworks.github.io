Mews & Woofs: Testing a Playful Pet-Sound Idea

Edoworks - September 15, 2026

Before investing in a full iOS product, we are testing whether pet owners engage with a small, playful interaction about sounds and context.

The hypothesis

Pet owners may enjoy turning an everyday sound into a memorable moment. The value we are testing is curiosity and shared play, not accurate translation of animal language.

What the experiment does

The concept preview presents fictional interpretation cards and a small interaction built around choosing a pet and a moment’s context. It is designed to be quick to try on a phone or desktop.

For this experiment, there is no account, microphone permission, audio upload, or required cloud service. Beta signup, App Store access, and TestFlight access are not open.

What it does not claim

  • It does not translate animal language.
  • It does not diagnose illness or provide veterinary or behavioral advice.
  • It does not establish scientific accuracy, adoption, or product-market fit.
  • Apple Intelligence is not required for this web experiment.

What we will learn

We will look for meaningful interaction rather than page views alone: whether visitors complete the sample, replay it, provide voluntary feedback, or ask to try a future version. A weak result is useful evidence too; it prevents unnecessary product investment.
