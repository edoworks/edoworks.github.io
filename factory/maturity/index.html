Methodology

The factory maturity level is the minimum across six independently-measured dimensions. A factory is only as mature as its weakest measured gate. A weighted average would let a strong dimension mask a failing one — so the aggregate is always the floor, never the average.

Each dimension is scored on a 0–3 scale:

L0 — manual
Dimension is unmeasured, ad-hoc, or has no automation.
L1 — instrumented
Dimension is measured but not gated; data exists but does not block.
L2 — gated
Dimension is measured and enforced as a gate; failure downgrades the aggregate.
L3 — sustained
Dimension is gated and stable at L2+ for the sustained window (14 days).

If any dimension is stale (input older than its max-age) or unknown (no source data), the aggregate is NOT COMPUTABLE regardless of other dimensions' levels. The engine never publishes a level when a dimension is stale or unknown — it reports NOT COMPUTABLE and the dimension's freshness status.

The maturity engine runs in shadow mode for 30 days after the initial deployment. During shadow, the engine computes the level and logs it but does not publish a public maturity badge. This page will display NOT COMPUTABLE until the shadow window completes and the engine is promoted to publication.
