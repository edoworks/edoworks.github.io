Mews & Woofs

A playful concept about noticing pet sounds, adding context, and making imaginative interpretation cards. It is entertainment and reflection, not literal translation, scientific analysis, behavioral diagnosis, or veterinary advice.

What this experiment tests

Can pet owners complete a small, low-friction interaction and want to see more? The prototype does not require an account, microphone, audio upload, or payment.

The live prototype is hosted temporarily on ChatGPT Sites while the factory evaluates interest. It is not the shipped iOS product or the canonical source repository.
