Veilsort

productivity

On-device email analysis via Apple Foundation Models. Revenue not yet instrumented.

Problem

Email is the primary vector for scams, phishing, and high-stakes miscommunication. Analyzing an email for hidden risks, deadlines, and safe reply strategies currently requires manual effort or pasting sensitive content into cloud AI services.

Audience

People who handle sensitive, regulated, or confidential emails — small business owners, retirees managing finances, startup founders reviewing legal notices. Privacy is essential: content must not leave the device.
